Hi, thanks for stopping by! I’m a UX designer with a strong foundation in research, interaction design, and project management. Driven by curiosity, I enjoy turning complex problems into solutions that actually make sense in people’s lives. I like working where user needs, business goals, and emerging technologies meet, especially when sustainability and long-term value are part of the equation. I’m comfortable taking ownership, asking the right questions, and turning insights into clear, thoughtful design decisions, with a growing interest in how AI and design can create more responsible, human-centered products.
